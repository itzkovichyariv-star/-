## Active-control video series

`active-control-videos/` contains five narrated English explainer videos ("The Science Behind Cannabis"), their scripts and the build pipeline. They are a neutral, mechanism-only active control condition based on Mechoulam, Hanuš, Pertwee & Howlett (2014), *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*. See `active-control-videos/README.md`.
